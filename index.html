Theia

People living with dementia, and people who are just forgetful, lose track of ordinary things. The glasses are on the desk. The earbud case is by the keyboard.

Theia is a tabletop turret. You ask for the object. It looks around the room, circles it on screen, and gives the angle a laser can point to.

You say what you are looking for, or type it. When you finish speaking, the search starts. A phone camera takes seven photos. Theia picks the best match, draws a mark on it, and shows azimuth and elevation in degrees.

Who it is for

People living with dementia, and people who are just forgetful. The glasses are on the desk. The earbud case is by the keyboard.

Someone else in the room asks for the glasses, keys, or medication.

A clinic closet or a shared desk. Same question: find this.
