Tabletop turret

Theia

Ask for an object. Theia circles it and gives a laser the angle.

People living with dementia, and people who are just forgetful, lose track of ordinary things. The glasses are on the desk. The earbud case is by the keyboard. Asking the group chat does not put a point on the object.

You say what you are looking for, or type it. A phone camera takes seven photos. Theia picks the best match, circles it, and shows azimuth and elevation in degrees.

Where it helps

Someone who is forgetful, or living with dementia

The glasses and the earbud case are still in the room. They ask for the object. Theia circles it and shows azimuth and elevation, so the point is on the thing itself.

A caregiver or a clinic closet

A caregiver, or a closet of lookalike supplies, needs one named item. Ask for it. Seven photos, one circle, and the angle from the turret.

A shared desk

Glasses and an earbud case trade places on a desk other people use. Ask once. Theia circles the match and shows azimuth and elevation.
