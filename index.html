Theia

People living with dementia, and people who are just forgetful, lose track of ordinary things. The glasses are on the desk. The earbud case is by the keyboard. Asking the group chat, or opening another app, does not put a point on the object.

That is why we built Theia, a tabletop turret. You ask for the object. It looks around the room, circles it on screen, and gives the angle a laser can point to.

You say what you are looking for, or type it. When you finish speaking, the search starts. A phone camera takes seven photos. Theia picks the best match, draws a mark on it, and shows azimuth and elevation in degrees.
