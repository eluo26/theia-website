What you see

  1. You speak. When the sentence ends, the search starts. Typing still uses Find it.
  2. The camera sweeps. Seven photos cover the desk or the room.
  3. One object is circled. If it shows up more than once, Theia keeps the strongest view.
  4. The laser gets an angle. Azimuth and elevation are the point in the room.

The same question, more rooms

We started with people living with dementia, and with anyone who misplaces glasses, keys, or medication. A caregiver, a clinic closet, or a shared desk can ask the same thing: find this.
